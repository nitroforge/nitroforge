## About

We make **Mario Kart Nitro**, a Mario Kart Wii mod for Dolphin, and the tools around it. Everything here is open source and built for the community.

## Projects

### <img src="assets/i-rocket.svg" width="22" alt="" />&nbsp;&nbsp;[NitroLauncher](https://github.com/nitroforge/NitroLauncher)

The one-click launcher. It installs the mod, keeps it current and starts Dolphin. Includes a Mii editor and a performance mode.

## Play in three steps

1. **Download** `MarioKartNitro.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/nitroforge/NitroLauncher/releases/latest).
2. **Point it** to your Dolphin and your own Mario Kart Wii disc image.
3. **Press Play.** The launcher downloads the mod the first time and updates it after that.

You need Windows 10 or 11 and Dolphin with Riivolution support. We do not provide the game.
